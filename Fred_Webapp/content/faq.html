Frequentry Asked Questions

Q:  There is a site on the FRED map close to my home, but why can't I find any fossils there?
A:The New Zealand Fossil Record File is a scheme for recording the localities of collection or occurrence of fossils relevant to stratigraphic or paleontological research. Most such occurrences are of scientific interest only: many are of rock samples collected for extraction of microfossils (e.g. foraminifera, pollen and spores) and there is little or nothing to be seen "on the ground". For localities where fossils may be seen and possibly collected, it is best to contact a local museum or rock and mineral club.
